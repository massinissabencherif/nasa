# Earth link — giving the wait a content (15 Nov)

**Observed (Massi, rehearsal):** once a report left, the Earth link showed a
countdown and nothing else for 40 minutes. The screen did not answer the
question it exists for: what does the crew do until Earth replies?

**Change:** on each report card, (1) what Earth reads next to what is on
board now; (2) an onboard plan ticked by a person; (3) demo rules that say
when to act without the reply (critical bands already chosen in
`demo_rules.json`, plus a second crew member turning red), with a red banner
that is never folded; (4) an update the crew reads before sending; (5) a
simulated reply built from what Earth actually received.

**Basis:** product inspiration from spaceflight practice — flight rules
decided in advance, Earth-independent medical operations (NASA ExMC/EIMO),
crews compressing messages under delay in analogue missions. These come
from the team's research notes, not from sources re-read for this change.
Local hypothesis for the layout. No user test was run. Nothing here is a medical
protocol.

**Trade-off:** more on one card; the report text moved into a fold (it is
secondary to the values, which stay visible).

**Smallest useful check:** a first-time viewer, after the Sofia scenario,
can say what the crew must do now, whether Earth's information is current,
and in which case they must not wait — within one minute, without help.
