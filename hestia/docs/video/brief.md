# HESTIA — 30 s NASA Space Apps video — brief (v0, before direction choice)

## Question (one viewer, one viewing)
"A Mars base has no doctor and Earth is 40 minutes away: what does HESTIA do?"
Expected answer: *It lets the crew check their own health, read their food, and decide — on board, offline,
and tells Earth only when needed.*
